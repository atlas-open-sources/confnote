AI credits terms

What you buy

AI credits pay for the confnote Concierge's AI work. Each step the Concierge takes uses credits based on the model and the amount of text it reads and writes. Your balance is shown in the account menu.

Delivery and your right to cancel

Credits are delivered to your account immediately after payment. Because they are digital content supplied at once, you ask us to deliver them straight away and you agree that you lose your 14-day right to cancel when you buy them. We ask for this consent at checkout and record it with your order.

No refunds

All credit purchases are final. Credits are non-refundable, including credits already used for AI work. This does not affect any rights you have if a payment was taken in error or the service does not work as described.

Expiry

Unused credits don't expire while your account is open.

Payments

Payments are processed by PayPal. We never see your card or PayPal login.
