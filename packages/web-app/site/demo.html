Metered AI you pay for with PayPal

confnote plans your conference days with an AI Concierge. Every AI step is metered by the Mochi Router wallet and paid for with PayPal: a free trial, credit packs, a card saved with Card Fields, and automatic top-ups. Recorded live on staging.confnote.si against the PayPal sandbox.

    confnote → Mochi Router gateway → wallet: meter the step → the model · out of credit: 402 → PayPal order / saved card → credits

    PayPal

    Orders v2 checkout for credit packs, Card Fields to save a card without a PayPal login, and server-side auto top-up against the vaulted card under a monthly cap.

    Bryntum

    The conference day, room by room: parallel talks side by side in the Planner. A pick there is a pick in confnote's own agenda and the other way round, with clashes shown in both.

    AG Grid

    Every event from our sources in one filterable grid, and the AI-usage grid that lists each metered step with its cost and the balance after it.
